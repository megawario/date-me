Why this exists

Dating apps flatten people. This makes things weird again.

I keep hearing friends talk about dating fatigue and how hard it feels to make a real connection—especially at the start. I feel it too. Apps can be awkward, and one tidy profile rarely shows how multidimensional a person actually is.

So this is my attempt to take matters into my own hands: a physical card that starts conversations in the real world, with a playful little website behind it. I can hand it out, friends can share it, and whatever happens next can be funny, human, romantic, or none of the above.

The only downside is making several profiles instead of one. But that is also the point: have fun with it and let the different parts of your personality shine.
